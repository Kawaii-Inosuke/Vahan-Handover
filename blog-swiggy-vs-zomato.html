Order Volume: Kahan Zyada Orders Milte Hain?

Order volume seedha aapki earning se connected hai. Jitne zyada orders, utni zyada earning. Is maamle mein:

  • Swiggy: Instamart (grocery) aur Genie (pickup-drop) ki wajah se non-meal-time par bhi orders milte rehte hain. Yeh ek bada advantage hai.
  • Zomato: Pure food ordering platform, isliye peak hours (lunch aur dinner) ke baad orders drop hote hain. Lekin 800+ cities mein presence matlab Tier-2/3 cities mein better coverage.
  • Conclusion: Metro cities mein Swiggy ke non-food services ki wajah se orders zyada consistent hote hain. Smaller cities mein Zomato ka wider presence faydement hai.

Final Verdict: Swiggy ya Zomato?

Ek line mein answer nahi hai. Yahan situation-wise recommendation hai:

  • Metro city mein ho: Swiggy slightly better hai, Instamart orders ki wajah se income more consistent.
  • Tier-2 ya Tier-3 city mein ho: Zomato ka wider coverage kaam aata hai.
  • Maximum earning chahiye: Dono join karo, simple.
  • Pehli baar join kar rahe ho: Jo bhi aapki city mein zyada popular hai, wahan se shuru karo.

Sabse smart move yeh hai ki dono platforms par simultaneously registered raho. Zomato aur Swiggy dono allow karte hain. Is tarah earning maximize hoti hai aur slow days ka risk khatam hota hai.

FAQs: Swiggy vs Zomato Delivery Partner Ke Baare Mein Sawaal

Everything you need to know before you start. Still have a question? Vahan.co par apply karo, hamari team aapko call karke sab samjha degi.

Kya main Swiggy aur Zomato dono pe ek saath kaam kar sakta hoon?

Haan, dono platforms simultaneously use karne mein koi restriction nahi hai. Aur yeh sabse common strategy bhi hai zyada earn karne ke liye.

Swiggy Instamart aur regular food delivery mein kya fark hai?

Instamart grocery delivery hai, grocery store se customer ke ghar tak. Yeh orders typically chhote aur frequent hote hain, aur non-meal-time par bhi aate hain.

Kaun sa platform jaldi payment karta hai?

Dono weekly payment karte hain directly bank account mein. Kuch markets mein daily payout option bhi available hai.

Kya Zomato Gold ya Swiggy One se riders ko koi benefit milta hai?

Ye customer subscription programs hain. Delivery partners ke liye direct benefit nahi hota, lekin in programs se order volume badhta hai, jo indirectly earning improve karta hai.

Nayi city mein shift hone par kya karna padega?

Dono platforms mein city change process hoti hai. Apne rider support se baat karo aur new city mein documents re-verify karwa lo.
